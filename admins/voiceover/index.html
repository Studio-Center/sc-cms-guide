AI voiceover

The AI voiceover plugin lets customers order a voiceover from your website. They pick one of your approved voices, paste their script, can hear the first lines free, see the price as they type, and pay with Stripe. The audio is made with ElevenLabs, using the voices in your own ElevenLabs account.

You’re selling your own service: customers never get access to your ElevenLabs account.

Before you start

Section titled “Before you start”
  • Talent agreements. Every voice you sell must have the talent’s signed agreement to use their voice with AI: how they’re paid, and how they can withdraw. The plugin won’t put a voice on sale until you’ve noted where the agreement is kept and when it was signed.
  • ElevenLabs. Check that your ElevenLabs plan allows commercial use of the audio you sell. Under Developers → API keys, create a key with access to text to speech and voices.
  • Stripe. You need a Stripe account. Under Developers → API keys, copy the secret key (or create a restricted key with write access to Checkout Sessions and read access to Events).
  • Content rules. Publish a page that says what scripts you accept, for example at /voiceover-rules. Customers confirm they follow it before paying.

Set up the plugin

Section titled “Set up the plugin”
  1. Go to Extensions, install AI voiceover, and approve its permissions.
  2. Open its settings and fill in:
    • ElevenLabs API key and Stripe secret key. They’re never shown again; leave a field blank later to keep its key.
    • Price per word and Minimum price. An order costs the number of words times the price per word, and never less than the minimum.
    • Longest script (words): longer scripts are refused, and the customer is asked to contact you.
    • Most characters to record a day: a safety limit on your ElevenLabs use. When it’s reached, free previews stop until the next day (UTC).
    • Email new orders to: the addresses that hear about each paid order.
    • Content rules page: the address of your rules page.
    • Listen before customers get their audio: on by default. Staff check each recording before the customer can download it.
    • Keep orders for (days): orders, their scripts, and their audio are removed after this many days. Checkouts that were never paid go after a week.
  3. In Stripe, go to Developers → Webhooks, add an endpoint at https://<your site>/api/v1/ext/voiceover/webhook, and choose the events checkout.session.completed and checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded. Then payments made by slower methods are recorded too.

The plugin’s status in Extensions says “Needs attention” and why, until the keys, a voice, and the order emails are set.

Add voices

Section titled “Add voices”

Go to Extensions → AI voiceover → Voices and choose Add a voice:

  • Name customers see and a short Description.
  • Code: a short name such as warm-female. It can’t be changed later.
  • ElevenLabs voice ID: in ElevenLabs, open Voices, open the voice, and copy its ID. The voice must be in your own account.
  • Model: Multilingual v2 sounds most natural. Turbo and Flash are faster and cost less.
  • Sample recording address: an MP3 that plays on the order form, such as a file from your media library. It must start with https://.
  • Price per word: leave it empty to use the price in the settings.
  • Talent agreement and Agreement signed on: needed before the voice can be sold.
  • On sale: untick it to stop selling the voice without removing it.

Add the order form to a page

Section titled “Add the order form to a page”

In the editor, add an AI voiceover order block. You can change its heading, introduction, and the label of the pay button. It lists the voices on sale, each with its sample.

Customers fill in their script, name, and email, and tick that the script is theirs to use. Hear the first lines (free) reads about the first 200 characters, up to five times a day for each visitor. Continue to payment takes them to Stripe.

Try it before going live

Section titled “Try it before going live”

Run one order from start to finish with Stripe’s test mode before you take real payments:

  1. In Stripe, switch on Test mode and copy the test secret key (it starts with sk_test_). Put it in Stripe secret key, and add the webhook in test mode too.
  2. Keep your real ElevenLabs key: test orders are recorded for real and use your ElevenLabs characters, so use a short script.
  3. Order from the page with the order form. On Stripe’s page, pay with the test card 4242 4242 4242 4242, any future expiry date, and any CVC.
  4. Check that:
    • you come back to the order page, and it says the payment went through;
    • the customer email and the new order email arrive;
    • the recording appears on the order’s screen, and you can listen to it;
    • after Release to customer, the order page plays it and Download the MP3 works;
    • Hear the first lines (free) plays a preview.
  5. Then mark the test order as refunded, put your live Stripe key (sk_live_…) in Stripe secret key, and add the webhook in live mode.

What happens after an order

Section titled “What happens after an order”
  1. The customer gets an email with a link to their order page. That link is the only way to reach the order, so they should keep it.
  2. Once Stripe confirms the payment, the addresses in Email new orders to get an email, and recording starts. Long scripts are recorded in parts and joined into one MP3 file.
  3. When the recording is done, staff get an email. Under Extensions → AI voiceover → Orders, open the order and download the Recording to listen to it. Then:
    • Release to customer: the customer’s order page now has a player and a Download the MP3 link.
    • Record again: fix the script (for example, spell a name the way it sounds) and save. The old recording is replaced; the price stays the same.
  4. If Listen before customers get their audio is off, the recording is released as soon as it’s made.

The customer’s download link lasts a few hours, and a new one is made each time they open their order page, so a link copied from an old page soon stops working.

When the recording is released, the customer gets an email saying it’s ready, with a link to their order page. The links in both emails keep working.

When an order needs attention

Section titled “When an order needs attention”
  • Needs attention: ElevenLabs refused the script or the request, for example because your ElevenLabs account ran out of credits or the voice was removed. Staff get an email with the reason. Fix the cause, then use Record again.
  • Waiting for tomorrow’s character limit: the day’s Most characters to record a day was reached. The order is recorded after midnight (UTC). To record it sooner, raise the limit and use Record again.
  • Refunds: refund the payment in Stripe, then choose Mark as refunded on the order’s screen. The recording is removed, and the customer’s page says the order was refunded.

Orders that were started but never paid stay as Awaiting payment for a week.

See also Shop and Extensions.
