The visual editor

Pages and posts are built from blocks: a heading, a paragraph, an image, a set of columns. The editor shows your real page, in your site’s theme, as you work on it.

The editor has three parts:

  • Left panel: Outline (the blocks on the page, as a tree) and Add (the block library).
  • Canvas in the middle: the page itself. Click a block to select it.
  • Right panel: the selected block’s settings (the inspector), or the page settings when nothing is selected.

Editing on the page

Section titled “Editing on the page”

When you open a page, you edit it as visitors will see it, with your site’s header and footer around it. A slim bar along the top holds everything else: go back to the list, add blocks, open the outline or the page settings, undo and redo, preview on a tablet or phone, and save or publish. If you’re coming from Concrete CMS, this works like edit mode there: the page is the editor.

Adding blocks

Section titled “Adding blocks”

Move the pointer over the page. A round + appears in the margin above and below the block you’re pointing at, and at the start and end of sections, columns, and accordion items. An empty column shows Add block here. Click a + and the block library opens on the left, showing only the blocks that fit in that spot. Click one to add it there. New text blocks are ready to type into right away; other blocks open their settings so you can pick an image, a link, and so on. + Add block in the top bar adds after the block you’ve selected, or at the end of the page.

The library groups blocks into Text, Media, Layout, Embeds, and Lists and more, with the blocks you added most recently at the top under Recently used. The links under the search box jump to a group, or to Patterns at the end. Type in the search box and press Enter to add the first match.

Block Use it for
Heading A section title.
Text Paragraphs with bold, italic, and links.
List Bulleted or numbered items, one per line.
Quote A quotation or testimonial, with who said it.
Image A photo or illustration, with alt text and a caption. Optionally a link, “Enlarge when clicked”, or a second image shown on hover.
Gallery Several images as a grid, a masonry wall, or a slider. Optionally shows captions and opens images larger when clicked.
Button A call to action: a link styled as a button.
Embed A video, map, or post from YouTube, Vimeo, Wistia, Simian, Google Maps, Facebook, or Instagram. Paste a link or the embed code.
Divider A horizontal line.
Spacer Extra vertical space.
Section Groups blocks, with a width (normal, wide, full) and a background.
Columns Side-by-side columns that stack on small screens.
Accordion Questions or sections that open and close, such as an FAQ.
Audio / Playlist An MP3 with a player; a playlist groups several.
Popup A button that opens blocks (or a synced pattern) in a popup.
Flip card An image that turns over when clicked to show the blocks on its back.
Carousel Image slides with a name over each that move sideways; a click opens more in a lightbox.
Feature An icon or small image with a title, a few words, and a link.
Content list The latest posts, testimonials, or any content type, or chosen pages side by side.

Extensions can add more blocks, such as a contact form or a Buy button. They appear in the library while the extension is active.

Special blocks

Section titled “Special blocks”
  • Accordions. Each item has a title (double-click to edit it) and holds any blocks. Turn on First item starts open or Only one open at a time. Choose the Title heading level that fits your page: one level below the heading above the accordion.
  • Embeds. Paste the address of the video or map, or the whole embed code the site gives you. Only the address is kept. The Title is required: it’s what screen readers announce. Pick a Shape, or Fixed height for maps and Simian reels. Extra height in pixels adds room under the shape, for players that list more videos below the picture (like a Simian playlist). Links from other sites are refused.
  • Audio. Under Style, Player shows play, time, and volume; Button shows a round play/pause button beside the title, for short clips such as on-hold messages.
  • Popups. The popup opens when visitors click the button and closes with Close, Esc, or a click outside. Popups can’t open by themselves after a delay or when someone is about to leave; that needs scripts, which sc-cms doesn’t add to your pages.
  • Flip cards. Choose the Front image, and optionally a Front image on hover. Put any blocks on the back (text, an image, a button). Visitors click the card to turn it over and click the back to turn it again; links and buttons on the back still work. Fill in Describe the front for screen readers. In the editor the back shows under the front, so you can edit it.
  • Carousels. Add a Slide for each image: choose its Image and type the Text over the image (a name, for example). Put the blocks the lightbox should show inside the slide, such as a heading and an audio player, or, with no blocks, fill in Show this page instead to show a page of the site (or another website) in it. Visitors click a slide to open its lightbox, move to the next one with ‹ and ›, and close it with ×, Esc, or a click outside. In Design mode choose Slides at once (and on phones), Go round endlessly, the arrows and dots, and whether it moves by itself (visitors then get a Pause button).
  • Columns. Pick a layout (two equal, narrow + wide, wide + narrow, sidebar + wide, wide + sidebar, three, four, or six equal), or type your own widths out of 12 under Custom widths, like 3 + 9 or 2 + 8 + 2. You can have up to six columns, and the widths must add up to 12. Choosing more widths adds empty columns; the editor never deletes one, so delete columns you don’t need yourself. Stack columns on chooses whether columns stack on tablets and phones, or only on phones.
  • Galleries. Columns goes from 1 to 8. Phones show two images (one for a single column), tablets up to four, and wide screens all of them. For the slider it’s how many images show at once.
  • Content lists. Choose The latest items of a content type (optionally only one category or tag, in the order you pick), or Items I choose. Quotes suits testimonials. Side by side shows the full content of the chosen pages, for comparisons. Lists show published items in the page’s language and update when you publish.
    • To show only some items, choose a field under Only items where (for example Style or Language) and a value under is or includes. It works together with the category and tag filters.
    • Show page numbers turns How many into the number per page and adds previous, next, and numbered links under the list.
    • Show as a slider (Design mode) puts the items in one row that visitors swipe or scroll, which suits rotating testimonials. Sliders don’t move by themselves.
  • Animation (Design mode). When it scrolls into view fades or slides a block in as visitors scroll to it. Visitors whose device asks for less motion, and browsers that don’t support it, simply see the block.

Editing text in place

Section titled “Editing text in place”

Double-click any text to type into it. A small toolbar appears under it (above it near the bottom of the window):

  • B for bold, I for italic, U for underline (or Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, and Ctrl+U), and S for strikethrough.
  • Size (Small, Large, Extra large, Huge), Font (the site’s body or heading font, or one of the families from the design settings: sans-serif, humanist sans, geometric sans, serif, old-style serif, monospace, or condensed tall capitals; each is shown in its own font in the menu), and Color: select some words, then pick one. Color offers your site’s own colors first (text, muted, accent…), then Use custom color for any other, and Default color to take a color off again. Sizes grow with the text around them, and Huge shrinks a little on phones. Words in a site color change with it if you change the site’s colors later; a custom color stays exactly that color.
  • The link button (or Ctrl+K) adds a link: type an address like /contact or https://…, or pick one of your pages. Tick Open in a new tab for links to other sites.
  • Clear formatting turns the selected words back into plain text (links stay).
  • On Text, Heading or List turns the paragraph into one. On a heading, choose its level.
  • Done (or Esc) finishes.

You can write a page without reaching for the mouse:

  • In a heading, Enter starts a new Text block under it, ready to type in. Shift+Enter breaks the line instead.
  • In text, Enter starts a new line. Press Enter on an empty last line to start a new Text block.
  • In an empty text block, Backspace removes it and takes you back to the end of the block before.
  • In an empty text block, type / to open the library in its place. Type part of a block’s name and press Enter to add it.

Esc, or clicking elsewhere, stops typing. Headings take bold, italic, and links too. When you paste from Word or Google Docs, bold, italic, and links come along; other styling is left behind so the page keeps your site’s look.

Arranging blocks

Section titled “Arranging blocks”

Point at a block to see its toolbar:

  • Edit opens its settings on the right.
  • The arrows move it up or down; Duplicate makes a copy; the bin deletes it (Undo brings it back).
  • ⋯ has more: design and layout options, selecting the block around it, adding a block before or after, and saving it as a pattern.

To move a block somewhere else, drag it by the dotted handle at the left of its toolbar. A line shows where it will land, even inside another column or section. With the keyboard, select a block in Outline and use Alt+↑ and Alt+↓; Enter opens its settings.

Keys Action
Ctrl/⌘ + Z Undo
Shift + Ctrl/⌘ + Z, or Ctrl/⌘ + Y Redo
Alt + ↑ / ↓ Move the selected block
Ctrl/⌘ + D Duplicate
Delete Remove
Alt + F10 Go to the block toolbar
Esc Deselect or close

The header and footer are shared by every page, so they’re greyed out here. If you can change the site’s design, Edit header or Edit footer takes you to them.

Settings, modes, and layouts

Section titled “Settings, modes, and layouts”

Page settings holds everything about the page that isn’t on it: title, address, and parent page; fields, categories, and visibility; publishing, scheduling, and review; history, comments, translations, and AI suggestions. Block settings open in the same panel. On a wide screen the panel sits beside the page, which narrows to make room, so the block you’re changing stays in view. On a smaller screen it opens over the page. Click Pin to keep the panel open even when no block is selected.

Block settings are grouped so you don’t have to scroll through everything:

  • Only the settings that apply are shown. For example, slider options appear once Show as a slider is on, and Chosen items once a list shows items you choose. Settings you hide this way keep their values.
  • Settings come in sections you can open and close, such as Items, Only some items, Slider, Spacing, Colors, or Animation. The first section is open. The editor remembers which ones you open for each kind of block, and a closed section shows how many of its settings you’ve changed.
  • Blocks with many settings, such as a content list or a gallery, split them into Content, Style, and Advanced tabs (Advanced has saving as a pattern, and anchors and CSS classes in Developer mode). Smaller blocks, such as a heading, show everything on one panel.
  • Move up, Move down, Duplicate, Copy, and Delete are at the top of the block’s settings.
  • Spacing sets the space outside and inside the block on each side in one small grid.
  • A setting with a few short choices, such as Alignment, shows them as a row of buttons, so one click picks one.
  • Text in a block’s settings (a Text, Quote, or Feature block’s text, a List’s items) is edited as it looks, with B, I, and Link above the box, like on the page. A list shows one item per line.
  • Settings that point at something else, such as which fields to hide or which list a List filters block belongs to, offer a list to choose from.

Content, Design, and Developer choose how much the settings show: just words and pictures, also layout and style (including Visibility: hide on phones, tablets, or desktops), or also anchors and CSS classes. The screen buttons in the top bar (Desktop, Tablet, and Phone) preview the page at different screen sizes. Undo and Redo are the curved arrows next to them; hold the pointer over any icon to see its name.

Prefer the old layout with the outline and settings always showing? Click the panels button (three columns) at the right of the top bar. The editor remembers your choice on this computer, and you can switch back at any time. In that layout the page starts at the top: publishing and scheduling, comments, translations, and AI suggestions are in the settings column, under the page’s settings.

Copying blocks to other pages

Section titled “Copying blocks to other pages”

To reuse a block on another page, hover it, open More actions (⋯) and choose Copy to clipboard, or select it and press Ctrl+C. Copying a section or columns copies everything inside it. On any page, choose + Add block or a “+” on the page: your copies are listed first under Clipboard, newest first, with the page they came from. Click one to paste it, or press Ctrl+V to paste your newest copy where you are.

Each paste is a separate copy, so editing it doesn’t change the original. Images and files are shared, not duplicated. Your clipboard keeps your last 20 copies and follows you to other pages and later sessions; remove a copy with its ✕. A copy that can’t go in the chosen spot is greyed out with the reason.

Filters, demo players, and compare in lists

Section titled “Filters, demo players, and compare in lists”

Letting visitors filter, search, and sort. In a content list’s settings, use Visitors can filter by to add the fields visitors can tick, such as Gender or Union Eligibility. You can rename each one, change their order, and choose Filter options: Alphabetical. Turn on Visitors can search by name for a search box. Under Visitors can sort, tick the orders to offer; the list starts in its Order until a visitor picks another. Update the list as visitors choose applies each tick at once; otherwise visitors press Show results. To put the form in a side column, set Show the filters to “In a List filters block” and add a List filters block where it should appear (if the page has several lists, choose which one in its For the list setting). Filtered pages have their own addresses, so visitors can bookmark or share them, but search engines don’t index them.

Demo players. Choose a file field under Play a sound from this field (for voice talent, Showcase). Each item gets a player, and starting one pauses any other sound on the page.

Compare. Turn on Visitors can compare items. Each card gets a “Compare” box, and picks collect in a “Selected Compare Items” tray that follows the visitor from page to page. Compare opens the Compare page (/compare-results by default). That page needs a content list showing Items visitors picked to compare, laid out “Side by side”. Picks are kept only in the visitor’s browser.

Galleries and sliders

Section titled “Galleries and sliders”

Gallery: choose images or a media collection, so photos can be managed in the media library. Pick Grid, Masonry, or Slider and the number of columns. You can show only the first few images with a Load more button, add a hover effect (zoom, or the caption sliding in), and let visitors open images larger, with arrows, swiping, and a play button.

Sliders: any Gallery or Content list can be a slider. Set how often it changes (in milliseconds; 0 means never), slide or fade, arrows, dots, pause while the pointer is over it, and looping. Testimonials can show one at a time, in random order. Visitors can always pause a slider, and it never moves for people who’ve asked their device for less motion. In the editor, sliders stay still so you can edit them.

Custom code holds HTML and scripts for one-off embeds. Only administrators can add, change, copy, or delete it. Others see it locked, but can still edit the rest of the page around it.

Spacing and colors

Section titled “Spacing and colors”

Every block has a Spacing and colors panel in its settings:

  • Spacing: space above, below, and beside the block, inside or outside its edge, in pixels. Space outside can be negative, to pull a block closer to the one before it.
  • Colors: the text, link, and background color. Pick one of your theme’s colors, so the block follows the theme if it changes, or enter an exact color.
  • Border, rounded corners, alignment, and text size.

Backgrounds and videos

Section titled “Backgrounds and videos”

A Section can stretch edge to edge and have a background image or a background video. Background videos play silently on a loop. Visitors who’ve asked their device for less motion see the image instead, so give the section an image too. Add a shade to keep text readable, a minimum height, and a narrower, centered content width.

You can add more than one background video. The first one plays, unless you turn on Pick one at random on each visit: then each visitor gets one of the videos, chosen at random. In the editor, and for visitors whose browser doesn’t run JavaScript, the first video always shows. All of them use the section’s image as their still picture, so pick videos that look right with it.

To show a video people can play, add the Video file block and choose a video from the media library. Videos on YouTube or Vimeo go in the Embed block.

Patterns

Section titled “Patterns”

A pattern is a ready-made group of blocks. The library includes Hero, Testimonial, Pricing, Team, FAQ, and Contact, plus any your team has saved.

To save your own, select a block (often a Section) and use ⋯ → Save as pattern in its toolbar, or Save as pattern in the inspector:

  • Synced (the default): every page using the pattern shows the same content. Editing the pattern updates them all. Use Edit pattern to change it, or Detach to turn one copy into ordinary editable blocks.
  • Not synced: inserting the pattern makes an independent copy.

Problems

Section titled “Problems”

If a block has a problem, such as an image with no alt text, it’s listed above the page. Click the problem to jump to the block.
